-has been delayed by more than a month due to the ongoing FIFA worldcup.

In the first few weeks June of this year, the 9th floor has collectively been (imho) the most productive since I have been here.  This was brought to a close, primarily by the start of the world cup which is taking place in the US, Mexico, and Canada. It doesn't help that several games are happening in Dallas and Houston as well. 

Although we come in and do the bare minimum work: meetings, a few small seminars, and a small amount of work everyday; right around lunch we just head into the lounge to watch a game. Then after lunch, we have to go to someone's place to watch another game, followed by another. 

The round of 32 has just ended and it has been a great worldcup so far. The fact that more teams have made it into the cup has made it very dynamic and entertaining so far. For instance, Cape Verde almost beating Messi's Argentina or Egypt finally making it to the knockout stages. The amount of vested interest others have in soccer has also made me take it a bit more seriously than usual in my life to the point where I know more about the Premier League than any other point in my life.

At this point, France seems to be the most likely to win the cup but people have money on Argentina or England as well. They did on Brazil today before they got knocked out by Haaland's Norway. While they don't pay me enough to go to an actual game, I was at the Japan v Brazil Houston fan fest where I unfortunately watched Japan get knocked out by Brazil on the Big Screen while surrounded in a sea of yellow.

At least the US teams looks competent this year. Even if they had to get the [president to bribe FIFA]([https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/05/us/trump-fifa-balogun-world-cup.html?smid=url-share](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/05/us/trump-fifa-balogun-world-cup.html?smid=url-share "https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/05/us/trump-fifa-balogun-world-cup.html?smid=url-share")) to give them a better chance against Belgium. 

Anyways, it doesn't look like much theoretical physics is getting done until the world cup is over.
